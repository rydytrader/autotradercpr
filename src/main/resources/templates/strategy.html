Overview

One trade per day on the NIFTY current-month future. At the 09:20 close of the first 5-minute bar, the bot compares the futures close to session VWAP and buys one 1 OTM weekly option — CE if bullish, PE if bearish. No indicators, no scanner, no re-entry.

Orders are placed as real Fyers MARKET intraday orders. The single position is managed to a fixed points target, a bar-close SL, or a hard timed squareoff — whichever fires first.

Session flow

09:15
Market opens. GDFL futures ticks start flowing on NIFTY-I (continuous current-month contract, auto-rolls at expiry server-side). Session VWAP is delivered on every tick as AveragedTradedPrice — not computed locally.
09:20
First 5-min bar closes → trigger evaluated. close vs VWAP picks the side, the OTM strike is resolved, the option is subscribed on demand, and a MARKET BUY is sent to Fyers. Fires exactly once per day.
09:25 →
Position management begins. Every option tick checks the points target; every 5-min futures close updates the opposite-side counter for the bar-close SL.
15:15
Timed squareoff. Default. If still in position (or defensively still in PENDING_ENTRY), MARKET sell everything. Configurable via optionBuyingSquareOffTime.

The trigger

Evaluated at the 09:20 futures 5-min bar close. Fires exactly once per day. close == VWAP → no trade, state jumps straight to DONE_FOR_DAY.

Bullish · above VWAP
BUY 1 OTM CE
Futures close > VWAP. Bot buys the call at strike round(close/50)*50 + 50. Current-week Tuesday expiry (walked back one day if that Tuesday is an NSE holiday).
Bearish · below VWAP
BUY 1 OTM PE
Futures close < VWAP. Bot buys the put at strike round(close/50)*50 − 50. Same current-week Tuesday expiry resolution.

Exit rules

Three exit paths; whichever fires first wins. All exits are MARKET sells on the open option leg.

Target

  • Fires when option LTP ≥ entryPrice + optionBuyingTargetPoints (default 20 points).
  • Evaluated on every option tick — not bar-bound.

Stop loss · 2 back-to-back opposite closes

  • Long CE → two consecutive futures 5-min bars close < VWAP.
  • Long PE → two consecutive futures 5-min bars close > VWAP.
  • Counter starts from the bar after the trigger bar (09:25 onwards) and resets to 0 on any correct-side close.
  • Evaluated only at 5-min futures bar close — not on ticks.

Squareoff · hard flatten

  • At optionBuyingSquareOffTime (default 15:15 IST) the open leg is flattened at market.
  • Also catches a stuck PENDING_ENTRY as a defensive squareoff.

Position sizing

  • Quantity = optionBuyingLotsPerLeg × 65. NIFTY lot size is fixed at 65.
  • Order type — Fyers MARKET, side BUY on entry and SELL on every exit path.
  • Product — optionBuyingOrderType, default INTRADAY. OVERNIGHT is available for CNC / carry.

Data pipeline

  • Market data — 100% GDFL. NIFTY futures via the continuous identifier NIFTY-I. GDFL handles the expiry roll server-side; no operator config needed.
  • 5-minute bars — CandleAggregator.BUCKET_MINUTES = 5, bucketed by IST wall clock.
  • Session VWAP is delivered by GDFL as AveragedTradedPrice on every tick. Not computed locally.
  • Option subscription is on-demand. Zero option strikes are subscribed before 09:20. The single chosen strike is subscribed at trigger time.
  • Fyers is order-side only — login + order placement + fill confirmations. No Fyers market data used by this strategy.
  • Persistence — full FSM state at store/cache/option-scalping-state.json (legacy filename; class is still OptionScalping). Bot restart mid-day resumes correctly.

What's NOT in OPTIONS BUYING

  • No indicators — VWAP is the only reference, and it comes from the feed.
  • No historical bar warm-up — the 09:20 trigger bar builds live from ticks.
  • No ATM pre-warm and no ±N strike subscription — one strike, subscribed on demand.
  • No re-entry after exit — one trade per day, then DONE_FOR_DAY.
  • No scanner, no watchlist filter, no CPR levels, no probability tiers.
  • No trailing SL and no tick-based SL — SL is bar-close only.

OPTIONS SELLING · VWAP-rejection premium seller

Second strategy on the same NIFTY current-month future. On every 5-min bar close where the bar's range spans VWAP AND the close is on one side of it, the bot sells an ATM option — CE if bearish, PE if bullish — collecting premium and running until the SL or squareoff fires. Multiple positions can be open simultaneously (one CE + one PE), and each side has its own session cap.

Orders are placed as real Fyers MARKET orders — SELL on entry, BUY-TO-COVER on every exit path. No per-position price target — SL or timed squareoff is the only way out.

OPTIONS SELLING · Session flow

09:15
Market opens. GDFL futures ticks flow on NIFTY-I. Session VWAP is delivered on every tick as AveragedTradedPrice — the same data feed OPTIONS BUYING uses.
09:20 →
Trigger evaluation begins at optionSellingStartTime (default 09:20). Every 5-min futures bar close is inspected: low ≤ vwap ≤ high AND close on one side of VWAP fires a new short entry on the corresponding ATM leg, subject to the serial-per-side rule and per-side cap.
Any bar
Per-position SL watch runs on every subsequent futures 5-min bar close. Two back-to-back opposite-side closes → BUY-TO-COVER market exit for that position. Counter starts from the bar after entry.
15:15
Timed squareoff. Default. Every open short (and defensively any stuck PENDING_ENTRY) gets a MARKET BUY-TO-COVER. Configurable via optionSellingSquareOffTime.

OPTIONS SELLING · The triggers

Both setups require the bar's range to span VWAP (low ≤ vwap ≤ high) — that's how we catch both crossover (body traverses VWAP and closes on the far side) and wick rejection (wick reaches VWAP but body closes back on the original side). Bars entirely above or entirely below VWAP do NOT trigger. close == VWAP is skipped as an edge case.

Bearish rejection · close < VWAP
SELL 1 ATM CE
Range spans VWAP and futures close < VWAP. Sell the call at strike round(close/50)*50 — same current-week Tuesday expiry as OPTIONS BUYING (walked back one day if that Tuesday is an NSE holiday).
Bullish rejection · close > VWAP
SELL 1 ATM PE
Range spans VWAP and futures close > VWAP. Sell the put at strike round(close/50)*50 — same current-week Tuesday expiry resolution.

OPTIONS SELLING · Exit rules

Two exit paths — SL or timed squareoff. There is no per-position option-price target — premium sellers keep their edge by running to expiration or SL.

Stop loss · 2 back-to-back opposite closes

  • Short CE → two consecutive futures 5-min bars close > VWAP (thesis broke — market wants to rally).
  • Short PE → two consecutive futures 5-min bars close < VWAP (thesis broke — market wants to fall).
  • Counter starts from the bar after the trigger bar and resets to 0 on any correct-side close.
  • Evaluated only at 5-min futures bar close — not on ticks.

Squareoff · hard flatten

  • At optionSellingSquareOffTime (default 15:15 IST) every open short is flattened at market via BUY-TO-COVER.
  • Also catches any stuck PENDING_ENTRY defensively — if the entry filled but the fill push hasn't landed, we place the exit anyway and let the tradebook reconcile.

OPTIONS SELLING · Concurrency + caps

  • Serial per side — at most ONE open CE + ONE open PE at any moment. Re-triggers while a same-side position is open are silently skipped (event-logged).
  • Per-side session caps — optionSellingMaxCeSellsPerDay (default 3) and optionSellingMaxPeSellsPerDay (default 3) independently limit new entries.
  • Rejections don't burn slots — the per-side counter only advances when the entry order returns ok with an orderId. Fyers rejections leave the counter untouched.

OPTIONS SELLING · Position sizing

  • Quantity = optionSellingLotsPerLeg × 65 per entry. Fixed at trigger time.
  • Order type — Fyers MARKET, side SELL on entry and BUY on every exit path (BUY-TO-COVER).
  • Product — optionSellingOrderType, default INTRADAY. OVERNIGHT is available for carry.
  • Cycle P&L for a short = (entryPrice − exitPrice) × qty minus per-cycle charges.
