Overview

The bot sells NIFTY weekly ATM options on their own 2-min timeframe. Each session locks in TWO legs at first bar close: the ATM CE and the ATM PE at the strike nearest to the first 2-min NIFTY close (rounded to 50). Every trade is a premium sell — bearish thesis, want premium to decay.

Each option's own session VWAP (the NSE-cumulative ATP the broker streams for that symbol) is the anchor. A 2-min bar that closes below VWAP seeds a trigger candle. If the very next 2-min bar closes below the trigger's low, the bot sells that leg. CE and PE run independent FSMs and can both be short at the same time; their combined worst-case loss is bounded by the portfolio-max-daily-loss cap.

One-shot per leg per day — once a leg's position closes (SL hit or timed squareoff), the FSM can seed again on later bars, but there's no re-entry latch. No target orders — every position is held until SL hit or the timed squareoff at the configured cutoff.

Session flow

  • 09:15 IST — market open. Bot subscribes NIFTY spot to the 2-min candle feed. No entries yet.
  • ~09:17 IST — first 2-min NIFTY bar closes. The bar's close price is snapshotted, rounded to the nearest 50-point strike, and the ATM CE + ATM PE symbols are resolved from the weekly chain. Both legs are subscribed to the 2-min feed and locked for the rest of the session.
  • From Trading Start (default 09:17 IST) onward. Every 2-min bar close on either option runs the trigger-candle FSM below. Only bearish sells fire.
  • Trading End (default 14:30 IST). No new entries after this time. Existing positions keep being managed.
  • Squareoff (default 15:25 IST). Any remaining open positions are flattened at market.

The FSM

One state machine per option leg. On every 2-min bar close for a leg, the bot reads that leg's session VWAP (Fyers ATP) and evaluates in this exact order:

S0 · seed
No active trigger. If this bar's close is < VWAP, it becomes the trigger candle. Otherwise stay in S0.
S1 · fire
Trigger active. If this bar's close is < trigger.low, SELL the leg. This check is unconditional vs VWAP — the fire happens regardless of where the close sits relative to VWAP.
S1 · promote
No fire, this bar closes < VWAP. The old trigger is dropped and THIS bar becomes the new trigger. Rolling behaviour — a fresh trigger seeds on every below-VWAP close until the very next bar fires it.
S1 · invalidate
No fire, this bar closes ≥ VWAP. The old trigger is dropped; no new trigger held. The state machine falls back to S0 and waits for the next below-VWAP close.

The trigger candle survives at most ONE follow-up bar. Either the follow-up fires or the trigger is dropped — there is no multi-bar "waiting on the same trigger." A bar close ≥ VWAP does not by itself invalidate anything; the fire check always runs first. Invalidation is strictly "immediate follow-through failed."

Reversals · L3 / H3

Bullish · Mean-reversion L3_REVERSAL
NIFTY wicks below L3 and recovers back above. Bot sells PE near L3, targets H3. SL = confirmation candle low + buffer.
Bearish · Mean-reversion H3_REVERSAL
NIFTY wicks above H3 and rejects back below. Bot sells CE near H3, targets L3. SL = confirmation candle high + buffer.

Breakouts · H4 / L4

Bullish · Trend continuation H4_BREAKOUT
NIFTY closes above H4, breaking out of the pivot band. Bot sells PE near H4, targets H5. SL = confirmation candle low + buffer.
Bearish · Trend continuation L4_BREAKDOWN
NIFTY closes below L4, breaking down through the floor. Bot sells CE near L4, targets L5. SL = confirmation candle high + buffer.

Stop loss

SL price = max(trigger.high, atmVwapMinSlPoints). The Min SL floor setting (default 10 option points, configurable in Settings → Portfolio Risk) is the floor when the trigger candle's high is smaller than the desired minimum SL — a tiny trigger candle would otherwise create a stop too tight to survive normal noise.

SL is monitored tick-based inside the strategy at the fast scheduler cadence (~500 ms). On any LTP tick where the option's live price reaches the SL level, the strategy fires a market BUY to close the short. No broker stop-loss order is placed — this guarantees a market fill even on gap moves or a thin order book.

No target — every open position is held until SL hit or timed squareoff. There is no take-profit level.

Risk management

  • Combined-risk gate — before every fire, the strategy projects existing exposed risk + (SL − entry) × qty for this new entry. If the projected total would breach portfolioMaxDailyLoss, the fire is rejected.
  • 50 % qty retry — when the full-lot fire would breach the portfolio cap, the bot retries once at half quantity (rounded to whole lots, min 1 lot). If the halved size still doesn't fit, the trade is skipped. No third try.
  • CE + PE simultaneous — both legs' FSMs run independently. Both can be short at the same time, but their combined worst-case loss (Σ (SL − entry) × qty across all open positions) is bounded by the same portfolio cap.
  • Realized-loss lockout — if today's closed losses exceed portfolioMaxDailyLoss, the strategy locks out for the rest of the session — no new fires on either leg.

Session windows

All three windows are configurable in Settings → ATM VWAP:

  • Trading Start (default 09:17) — the FSM runs from first bar close, but sells don't fire until this time. Applies to the fire gate; seed/promote/invalidate still update. Earliest fire opportunity is the 09:21 bar close (when the 09:19 trigger candle's low is broken).
  • Trading End (default 14:30) — no new entries after this time. Existing positions keep being managed until squareoff.
  • Squareoff (default 15:25) — any remaining open positions are flattened at market.

What's NOT in this strategy

  • No target orders — SL-hit or timed-squareoff exits only.
  • No re-entry after SL — once the leg's position closes, that FSM cycles fresh but there's no automatic re-fire on the same trigger.
  • No bullish setups — every fire is a premium sell.
  • No pivot levels, no ATR, no VWAP bands, no scanner filters. VWAP is the only anchor.
