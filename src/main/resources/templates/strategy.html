Overview

The bot trades NIFTY weekly options, but the source of truth is NOT the NIFTY spot chart — it's the option premium itself. Each session the bot locks in TWO option legs: the CE at NIFTY's H5 strike and the PE at NIFTY's L5 strike. It then computes Camarilla pivots for EACH option's own prior-day OHLC and monitors those option-premium 3-min candles for bearish setups on each option's own Camarilla.

Every trade is a premium sell — we sell the option we're monitoring when its own premium starts breaking down. The strategy is directionally biased toward premium decay: we fire only on bearish setups (L4 breakdown or H3 reversal on the option's own Camarilla), never on bullish setups.

Entries use a 3-min two-bar model: a confirmation bar prints (red bar wicked through the level and closed back below) and seeds a pending; the NEXT 3-min bar must close BELOW the confirmation's low to fire the sell. Stop loss is placed at the option's own next structural level (L3 for L4 breakdown, H4 for H3 reversal) as a broker order — additionally, if a 3-min bar closes at or above that SL level, the bar-close backup exits the trade.

Risk management is layered: per-day loss cap with a 50 %-qty retry when a full-size fire would breach the cap, portfolio exposure projection, and a daily squareoff time.

Camarilla Pivots

Computed nightly from the previous day's High, Low, and Close. The pivot range k = 1.1 × (H − L) sets the scale of all six trading levels:

H5
(H ÷ L) × C
extreme upside target
H4
C + k/2
breakout level
H3
C + k/4
upper reversal zone
L3
C − k/4
lower reversal zone
L4
C − k/2
breakdown level
L5
(L ÷ H) × C
extreme downside target

The L3–H3 band is the "neutral" core; price spending time inside it tends to mean-revert. The H4 / L4 levels mark the structural edges — a sustained break beyond them signals a directional regime change. H5 / L5 are extreme-move targets, reached only on strong-trend days.

The Two Setups

Both setups are bearish on the option premium — they fire when the option we're monitoring starts to lose value. Applied per option leg (H5 CE and L5 PE) on that option's OWN Camarilla, evaluated at each 3-min bar close.

Bearish · Structural breakdown L4_BREAKDOWN
Option premium (3-min bar) prints a red candle whose high ≥ L4 and whose close is < L4 of THIS option's own Camarilla. Seed a pending. If the NEXT 3-min bar closes below the confirmation's low, sell the option. SL = option's own L3, target = option's own L5 (both broker orders + 3-min close backup).
Bearish · Rejection H3_REVERSAL
Option premium (3-min bar) prints a red candle whose high ≥ H3 and whose close is < H3 of THIS option's own Camarilla. Seed a pending. If the NEXT 3-min bar closes below the confirmation's low, sell the option. SL = option's own H4, target = option's own L3 (both broker orders + 3-min close backup).

The bullish counterparts (L3_REVERSAL / H4_BREAKOUT) are intentionally not fired — buying premium is not the strategy's thesis. Only bar-close geometry + red-candle color qualify a confirmation; wicks alone don't. Skipping the rest of the old setup diagrams — the geometry is applied to the OPTION'S own price chart, not NIFTY's.

Reversals · L3 / H3

Bullish · Mean-reversion L3_REVERSAL
NIFTY wicks below L3 and recovers back above. Bot sells PE near L3, targets H3. SL = confirmation candle low + buffer.
Bearish · Mean-reversion H3_REVERSAL
NIFTY wicks above H3 and rejects back below. Bot sells CE near H3, targets L3. SL = confirmation candle high + buffer.

Breakouts · H4 / L4

Bullish · Trend continuation H4_BREAKOUT
NIFTY closes above H4, breaking out of the pivot band. Bot sells PE near H4, targets H5. SL = confirmation candle low + buffer.
Bearish · Trend continuation L4_BREAKDOWN
NIFTY closes below L4, breaking down through the floor. Bot sells CE near L4, targets L5. SL = confirmation candle high + buffer.

3-Min Two-Bar Entry Model

Every 3-min bar close on EACH monitored option premium runs the walk below. Two bars are required to enter — the confirmation bar prints the setup geometry, the trigger bar closes past that confirmation's low.

Fire
Two-bar entry trigger. If a pending confirmation exists on this option AND this 3-min bar's close < the confirmation's own low, sell the option at this close. Position size = lotsPerLeg × 65, possibly halved by the portfolio-risk retry. Broker SL order placed immediately at the option's own next structural level (L3 or H4). Target is monitored tick-by-tick — see Order Flow.
Detect
Fresh confirmation. If no open position AND no pending on this option, check bearish-only geometry against the option's own Camarilla:
• L4_BREAKDOWN — red 3-min bar whose high ≥ L4 AND close < L4.
• H3_REVERSAL — red 3-min bar whose high ≥ H3 AND close < H3.
Doji bars (close == open) are rejected. A match seeds a pending on this option; the NEXT bar's close is the trigger.
Expire
Age-out — a pending older than MAX_PENDING_BARS (3 bars ≈ 9 min at 3-min cadence) is dropped without trading. The confirmation went stale; the move it predicted didn't materialize in time.

Order Flow & Exit Logic

When the trigger fires, the bot places a single SELL order on the option that was being monitored (H5 CE or L5 PE). Position size = lotsPerLeg × 65, possibly halved by the portfolio-risk retry when a full-size fire would breach the daily loss cap. Both a broker SL order and a broker target order are placed at entry, at levels drawn from the option's own Camarilla.

  • Stop loss — broker-side SL order at option's own L3 (for L4_BREAKDOWN) or H4 (for H3_REVERSAL). Placed at entry, held on the exchange. Fires on any tick that reaches the level. Sole SL mechanism — no in-strategy backup; a broker rejection is logged and leaves the position unprotected until timed squareoff.
  • Target — tick-based, monitored inside the strategy at the fast scheduler cadence (~500 ms). On any LTP tick where the option's live price is at or below the target level (L5 for L4_BREAKDOWN, L3 for H3_REVERSAL), the strategy fires a market BUY to close the short. No broker LIMIT order — guarantees a fill even on a gap or thin book.
  • Timed squareoff — at the configured squareoff time (default 15:15 IST), all open positions are flattened regardless of SL/target state.

Risk Management

  • Portfolio exposure cap — before every fire, the strategy projects the combined exposed risk (existing open positions + this new fire's |entryFut − slFut| × ATM_DELTA × qty) and rejects if it would breach portfolioMaxDailyLoss.
  • 50 % qty retry — when the full-lot fire would breach the portfolio cap, the bot retries once at half lots (rounded to whole lots, min 1). If the halved size still doesn't fit, the trade is skipped. No third try — position size never fragments below half.
  • Realized-loss lockout — if today's closed losses exceed portfolioMaxDailyLoss, the strategy locks out for the rest of the session — no new fires on either instrument.
  • Trading window — new entries only fire between configured Trading Start (09:30 IST default) and Trading End (13:30 IST default). Existing positions continue to be managed until squareoff.

Real-Time Monitoring

The bot maintains two persistent Fyers WebSocket connections during market hours: one for market data (NIFTY spot + every subscribed option leg's LTP and OI) and one for order events (entry fills, modifications, cancellations). Sub-second SL/target checks run on every spot tick — no polling latency between a level breach and the exit order going out.

Two browser-side surfaces stream live state via Server-Sent Events: the navbar ticker (NIFTY LTP + ΔPct) and the Positions page (per-position LTP + MTM). All persistent state survives mid-day restarts and weekend gaps via JSON disk snapshots.
