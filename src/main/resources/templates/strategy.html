Overview

Options-selling strategy on NIFTY weekly CE + PE. On the first NIFTY spot tick after 09:15 IST, the bot captures the spot open, computes atmStrike = round(spot / 50) × 50, and picks the ATM CE and ATM PE as the tracked pair — no LTP scan, no premium proximity. Both legs are monitored independently on their own 3-min chart; either can be short at the same time (short straddle if both fire).

Entries fire on either a VWAP breakdown (bar straddles VWAP and closes below with Supertrend down) or a Supertrend flip down (ST goes green→red while price is below VWAP). Order side is SELL to open (MARGIN product). SL = Supertrend line at entry (finalUpper — above fill) — trailed down on every 3-min close where ST descends. Exit fires only when a 3-min bar closes above the current SL. Wicks are ignored (bar-close driven). No fixed target unless RR is set. Unlimited re-entries per leg (a stopped trade re-arms via VWAP_BREAKDOWN or ST-flip on any subsequent qualifying bar); hard timed squareoff flattens everything before market close.

Session flow

09:13
Pre-market subscribe. Bot fetches NIFTY spot's previous daily bar via Fyers /data/history to derive the anchor ATM from prev close, and subscribes ±N strikes for both CE and PE. In parallel, the near-month NIFTY futures contract is subscribed and its Supertrend(10, 3) on 3-min bars drives the header BIAS chip. Fires right after NSE's 09:12 pre-open window closes.
09:15
Spot open + ATM pair pick. First NIFTY tick sets spotOpen and atmStrike = round(spot / 50) × 50. ATM CE and ATM PE are chosen immediately — no LTP scan, no wait. Historical 1-min bars from Fyers are prepended so Supertrend ATR is valid from bar 1 today. FSM → ARMED.
09:18 →
Bar-by-bar evaluation. Every 3-min close for both legs is checked against both entry pathways. SL is also evaluated on the same 3-min close (bar close > slPrice → exit). No tick-level exits.
15:25
Timed squareoff. Default. Any open short leg is bought back at market; state transitions to DONE_FOR_DAY. Configurable via vwapStSquareOffTime.

Entry pathways

Two independent entry pathways per leg. Whichever fires first on a given 3-min close opens the position; both cannot fire on the same bar.

VWAP_BREAKDOWN
VWAP breakdown + ST aligned
Bar's low ≤ VWAP ≤ high (range straddled VWAP) AND close < VWAP AND Supertrend is down. Premium wicked VWAP as resistance and rolled over. ST already confirms the downside. Sell that option.
SUPER_TREND_FLIP_DOWN
Fresh green→red ST flip
Previous bar's ST was up AND this bar's ST is down AND close < VWAP. Premium had been in an uptrend, retested VWAP as support, then broke through and closed below — ST flips green→red. Sell at that close.

Skipped entries are logged. When a VWAP breakdown condition is met but ST is still green, an [WARNING] entry SKIPPED — VWAP breakdown but Supertrend not aligned event surfaces in the log so you can audit near-misses.

Exit rules

Bar-close-driven SL only. No tick-level exits. No fixed target — every exit path is a market buy-back to close the short.

Stop loss — Supertrend, trailed down

  • Initial SL = Supertrend line at entry (finalUpper — ABOVE fill for shorts). No fixed rupee buffer, no max-SL cap.
  • On every 3-min close where ST is still down and the line has fallen, SL ratchets DOWN to the new line (never widens). One [INFO] SL trailed event per update. Trail can descend BELOW fill on a winning trade — that just locks in more profit.
  • Exit fires when a 3-min bar closes above the current SL. Wicks are ignored (bar-close driven). Reason tag: TRAILING_SL_HIT if the trail had moved SL below initial (locked profit), else SL_HIT.
  • Self-healing re-entry — a stopped trade re-arms via the VWAP_BREAKDOWN or SUPER_TREND_FLIP_DOWN pathway on the next qualifying bar.

Target

  • No fixed target — the position RIDES the trailing SL. Theta decay + directional move against the sold leg is where the profit comes from.

Squareoff

  • At Squareoff Time (default 15:25 IST) every open leg is bought back at market. Also catches stuck PENDING_ENTRY as a defensive squareoff.
  • Portfolio loss kill switch — when combined live net P&L across enabled strategies drops below −Max Daily Risk, everything flattens and FSM transitions to DONE_FOR_DAY. Loss side only — profits ride freely.

Position sizing

  • Quantity per leg = Lots per Leg × 65. NIFTY lot size is fixed at 65.
  • Order type — Fyers MARKET, side SELL (−1) on entry and BUY (+1) on every exit path (buy back to close).
  • Product — MARGIN. Options SELLING requires SPAN + Exposure margin (~₹1-1.5 L per lot for ATM NIFTY weekly). Both legs (CE + PE) may be short simultaneously = short straddle; sized independently.

Data pipeline

  • Market data — 100% Fyers HSM WebSocket. NIFTY spot + option strikes. Binary FULL mode: LTP + ATP + volume + LTT + EFT + prev-close.
  • 1-min bars built locally from ticks (FyersMinuteBarBuilder). Bucketed by EFT / 60. Volume = sessionVol delta within bucket. First bucket of the day per symbol seeds startSessionVol = 0 so the opening auction print is counted.
  • 3-min bars — CandleAggregator.getHistory(sym, 3) aggregates the 1-min ring on demand. Listeners fire on every 1-min close; strategy's own alignment gate picks the right N-min boundary.
  • Session VWAP — pandas_ta cumulative Σ((H+L+C)/3 × V) / ΣV anchored to IST midnight, computed locally from the 1-min ring.
  • Supertrend(ATR, Multiplier) — canonical formulation, recomputed on every tick including the forming bar. Warmup pulls prior-session 1-min bars via Fyers /data/history so ATR is valid from 09:15.
  • Persistence — FSM + per-leg state at store/cache/vwap-supertrend-state.json, candle ring at store/cache/candle-aggregator-state.json. Mid-day restart restores chosen strikes, per-leg fill/SL/target, and re-runs warmup automatically.

What's NOT in this strategy

  • No GDFL — Fyers HSM WebSocket is the sole market-data source.
  • No fixed target — every winner rides the trailing SL until it hits.
  • No POINTS / ATR / max-SL cap modes — SL is always the Supertrend line.
  • No max-SL cap — actual loss on exit = (close of exit bar) − fill, which can exceed the initial displayed risk on wide bars.
  • No tick-level SL — wicks above the SL line are ignored, only 3-min bar close matters.
  • No partial exit — one-shot ride until SL or squareoff.
  • No premium-based strike selection — always ATM at 09:15 spot open.
  • Directional bias filter is opt-in — when enabled in settings, CE sells are skipped while NIFTY near-month futures Supertrend(10, 3) is bullish and PE sells are skipped while it's bearish. Fail-closed: if futures data is unavailable, all entries are blocked rather than firing blind. When disabled, trades fire regardless and the BIAS chip is informational only.
  • No portfolio profit cap — only the loss side kills the day.
  • No re-entry cooldown — every fresh signal on a WAITING leg is eligible.
