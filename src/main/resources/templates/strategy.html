Strangle + Adjustments · Overview

An intraday weekly options-selling strangle that rotates between NIFTY and SENSEX by weekday. Every session sells one CE + one PE strike whose LTP is closest to a target premium (₹50 for NIFTY, ₹120 for SENSEX by default). Per-leg SL is 100 % of the received premium.

If one leg hits SL, the strategy runs a recovery adjustment: close the SL-hit leg, then on the OPPOSITE side (a) BUY a deep-OTM hedge and (b) SELL a new leg at the target premium. Hedge + new sell form a defined-risk bear-call or bull-put spread — the hedge caps loss and unlocks broker margin.

One-shot rule: if the adjustment leg's own SL fires, close it and stop — no further recovery cycles. The other side (original short + hedge, if any) rides to timed squareoff at 15:15 IST. Analytics treats each session day as one trade (not one row per leg).

Instrument rotation

Settings-configurable per weekday. Each day picks one of NIFTY, SENSEX, or DISABLED. Defaults:

  • Monday, Tuesday → NIFTY (spot NSE:NIFTY50-INDEX, lot 65, strike step 50).
  • Wednesday, Thursday → SENSEX (spot BSE:SENSEX-INDEX, lot 20, strike step 100).
  • Friday → DISABLED (no trading).

The instrument chosen for the day is locked at 09:20 entry and persisted. A mid-session restart keeps the same instrument.

Entry (09:20 IST)

  • Fetch today's option chain from the resolved spot symbol (NIFTY or SENSEX).
  • Pick the CE strike whose LTP is closest to the target premium (₹50 for NIFTY, ₹120 for SENSEX). SELL 1 × lots.
  • Pick the PE strike whose LTP is closest to the target premium. SELL 1 × lots.
  • SL price for each leg = entryPremium × slMultiplier (default 2.0 — SL triggers when the option's premium reaches 2× the received premium, i.e. a 100 % loss of the received credit).
  • No target — legs are held until SL hit or timed squareoff.

Adjustment (SL recovery)

Triggered when either initial leg hits its 100 % SL. The adjustment side is the OPPOSITE of the SL-hit side (CE SL → new PE + PE hedge; PE SL → new CE + CE hedge).

  • Close the SL-hit leg at market.
  • BUY hedge FIRST — deep-OTM on the adjustment side, hedgeStrikesAway strike-steps further OTM from the strike about to be sold (default 10 steps = 500 pts for NIFTY, 1000 pts for SENSEX). Qty = base × hedgeQtyMultiplier (default 2×) for margin relief.
  • SELL new leg at the strike whose current LTP is closest to the target premium.
  • The hedge + new sell together form a defined-risk vertical spread (bear call or bull put): max loss is bounded by strike difference − net credit.

One-shot: if the adjustment leg's own SL fires, close it and stop. No further recovery cycles. The remaining shorts and hedges continue managed until squareoff.

Stop loss

SL price = entryPremium × slMultiplier. With the default multiplier of 2.0, a leg sold at ₹50 has SL at ₹100 — a 100 % loss of the received credit.

SL is tick-based inside the strategy at the fast scheduler cadence (~500 ms). On any LTP tick where the leg's live price reaches its SL level, the strategy fires a market BUY to close the short and trigger the adjustment cycle if eligible.

Hedges are BUYs — they have no SL and stay open until the timed squareoff at 15:15 IST (deep OTM, typically decays to near-zero).

Session windows

Both configurable in Settings → STRANGLE:

  • Entry Time (default 09:20) — strangle fires once at/after this time on eligible weekdays.
  • Squareoff Time (default 15:15) — every remaining open leg is flattened at market.

Analytics — day-as-one-trade

Each session day rolls up to one row for the Home hero metrics, equity curve, By-Instrument breakdown, and By-Day-of-Week breakdown. So a day with 5 legs (2 initial + 2 adjust + 1 hedge) but a net profit counts as 1 win, not 5. The Trade Log page still shows every individual leg for detail.

By Instrument bins P&L into NIFTY-day vs SENSEX-day totals (natural for the weekday rotation). By Day bins Mon–Fri.

What's NOT in this strategy

  • No recursive adjustment — one recovery per side per session, then stop.
  • No target orders — exits are SL, adjustment SL, or timed squareoff only.
  • No VWAP / bar-close FSM / trigger candles / ATR.
  • No re-entry after squareoff. Next day starts fresh at 09:20.
  • No weekend trading. Friday defaults to DISABLED but can be flipped to NIFTY or SENSEX in Settings if desired.
