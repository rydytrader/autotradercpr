Overview

Options-buying strategy on NIFTY weekly CE + PE. On the first NIFTY spot tick after 09:15 IST, the bot captures the spot open, subscribes ±N strikes, and picks the CE and PE trading closest to the configured target premium (default ₹250) as the tracked pair. Both legs are monitored independently on their own 3-min chart; either can be in position at the same time.

Entries fire on either a VWAP breakout (bar straddles VWAP and closes above with Supertrend up) or a Supertrend flip (ST goes red→green while price is above VWAP). SL is the entry candle low − buffer, capped at Max SL points. Target is 2× the effective risk. Unlimited re-entries per leg; a hard timed squareoff flattens everything before market close.

Session flow

09:10
Pre-market subscribe. Bot pulls NIFTY spot's prev close, computes an anchor ATM, and subscribes ±N strikes for both CE and PE. LTPs start flowing at 09:15 open with no lag.
09:15
Spot open captured. First NIFTY tick sets spotOpen. If actual ATM drifts outside the pre-subscribed range (a gap open), missing strikes are subscribed on the fly.
09:15 :15
Pair pick + warmup. Once LTPs have flowed for ~15 s, the CE and PE nearest to Target Premium are chosen. Historical 1-min bars from Fyers are prepended so Supertrend ATR is valid from bar 1 today.
09:18 →
Bar-by-bar evaluation. Every 3-min close for both legs is checked against both entry pathways. LTP ticks continuously monitor SL and target for any open leg.
15:25
Timed squareoff. Default. Any open leg is MARKET sold; state transitions to DONE_FOR_DAY. Configurable via vwapStSquareOffTime.

Entry pathways

Two independent entry pathways per leg. Whichever fires first on a given 3-min close opens the position; both cannot fire on the same bar.

VWAP_BREAKOUT
VWAP bounce + ST aligned
Bar's low ≤ VWAP ≤ high (range straddled VWAP) AND close > VWAP AND Supertrend is up. The candle wick tested VWAP as support and the ST trend already confirms the upside. Bar-body color is irrelevant.
SUPER_TREND_FLIP
Fresh red→green ST flip
Previous bar's ST was down AND this bar's ST is up AND close > VWAP. The scenario: price trading above VWAP, retraces to VWAP for support, then reversal candle breaks through the red ST band and closes beyond it — flipping ST to green. Enter at that close.

Skipped entries are logged. When a VWAP breakout condition is met but ST is still red, an [WARNING] entry SKIPPED — VWAP breakout but Supertrend not aligned event surfaces in the log so you can audit near-misses.

Exit rules

LTP-driven: SL and target are evaluated on every option tick, not at bar close. Whichever fires first wins. All exits are MARKET sells.

Stop loss

  • Structural SL = entryCandleLow − SL Buffer.
  • Capped at Max SL points below fill. If the structural SL is further than the cap, SL is clamped to fill − Max SL. A [INFO] SL CAPPED event is logged so you can spot which entries were clipped.
  • Fires when LTP ≤ SL price.

Target

  • Target = fill + Reward:Risk × (fill − effective SL). Default RR is 2.0 — a 1:2 payoff on the effective (post-cap) risk.
  • Fires when LTP ≥ target price.

Squareoff

  • At Squareoff Time (default 15:25 IST) every open leg is flattened at market. Also catches stuck PENDING_ENTRY as a defensive squareoff.

Position sizing

  • Quantity per leg = Lots per Leg × 65. NIFTY lot size is fixed at 65.
  • Order type — Fyers MARKET, side BUY on entry and SELL on every exit path.
  • Product — INTRADAY. Both legs (CE + PE) may be open simultaneously; each sized independently.

Data pipeline

  • Market data — 100% Fyers HSM WebSocket. NIFTY spot + option strikes. Binary FULL mode: LTP + ATP + volume + LTT + EFT + prev-close.
  • 1-min bars built locally from ticks (FyersMinuteBarBuilder). Bucketed by EFT / 60. Volume = sessionVol delta within bucket. First bucket of the day per symbol seeds startSessionVol = 0 so the opening auction print is counted.
  • 3-min bars — CandleAggregator.getHistory(sym, 3) aggregates the 1-min ring on demand. Listeners fire on every 1-min close; strategy's own alignment gate picks the right N-min boundary.
  • Session VWAP — pandas_ta cumulative Σ((H+L+C)/3 × V) / ΣV anchored to IST midnight, computed locally from the 1-min ring.
  • Supertrend(ATR, Multiplier) — canonical formulation, recomputed on every tick including the forming bar. Warmup pulls prior-session 1-min bars via Fyers /data/history so ATR is valid from 09:15.
  • Persistence — FSM + per-leg state at store/cache/vwap-supertrend-state.json, candle ring at store/cache/candle-aggregator-state.json. Mid-day restart restores chosen strikes, per-leg fill/SL/target, and re-runs warmup automatically.

What's NOT in this strategy

  • No GDFL — Fyers HSM WebSocket is the sole market-data source.
  • No trailing SL — SL is fixed at entry (below entry candle low, capped at Max SL).
  • No scanner, watchlist, or NIFTY-100 rotation — only the CE + PE chosen at 09:15 are traded.
  • No ST-flip exit — Supertrend flips are only used as entry filters and triggers, never as exit signals. Exits are LTP-driven SL / target / squareoff only.
  • No re-entry cooldown — every fresh signal is eligible; the leg-state gate is the only limiter.
