Risk Controls
Enable structural SL
ATR Period Buffer × ATR
Single-level extra buffer × ATR (R2/R3/R4, S2/S3/S4 only)
Apply single-level buffer to zone setups when CPR width < % (0 = disabled)
SL = anchor ± buffer × ATR(period). Anchored to the broken level (CPR, R1/PDH, R2, etc.). The single-level extra buffer is added only to setups that lack a zone-width cushion (R2/R3/R4, S2/S3/S4); zone setups (CPR, R1+PDH, S1+PDL, magnets) get their cushion from the zone width itself unless CPR width % is below the threshold above — then the zone is too tight to absorb a normal pullback, so the extra buffer is added to those zone setups too. Breakout detection still uses the full zone. ATR period also used for candle filters and volatility measures.
Skip trade if reward/risk < :1
Computed after SL and final target are determined. R/R = |target − entry| / |entry − SL|. Uses the final target (T2 for split trades) and the final SL (structural or default, whichever was picked). Default 1.0 means reject any trade where reward is less than risk.
Enable breakeven stop-loss
Trigger @ % of range → SL at entry ± × ATR
Range = entry (0%) → target (100%). When peak/trough reaches the trigger %, SL moves to entry ± buffer × ATR (locks in a small profit, never widens). For split-target trades, breakeven is suppressed until T1 fills, then activates on the remaining qty. Defaults: 61.8 / 1.
Stock Price vs EMA 20 — exit when stock's 5-min candle close is on the wrong side of its EMA 20 (LONG: close < EMA 20; SHORT: close > EMA 20)
Stock-side defensive-exit check, evaluated on every 5-min candle close (after the candle's EMA is stamped). Fires before SL / Target / Breakeven; cancels both OCO legs and places an exit market order. Reason recorded: PRICE_EMA_EXIT. NIFTY-side macro safety-net mirrors of this check live on the Index Alignment tab.
Trade Filters
Price: 5-min EMA trend — close above EMA 20 for buys, close below EMA 20 for sells
Gates entries by price position relative to the 5-min EMA 20. Buys require close > EMA 20; sells require close < EMA 20. Failing the gate rejects the trade. Fail-open when EMA 20 is still warming up.
Reject entries when today's range has consumed most of the 14-day daily ATR
Reject when today's TR ≥ × daily ATR(14) (0 = off)
Computes today's True Range (max of today high−low, |today high−prev close|, |today low−prev close|) and compares to the stock's 14-day daily ATR (from bhavcopy daily OHLC). When the ratio crosses the threshold, both buy and sell signals are rejected — the daily move is near-spent and remaining range is likely too small for the configured R/R. Default 0.85 = reject when 85% of the daily ATR is already consumed. The scanner card's ATR chip uses the same threshold for its green/red colour. Signal Trail bucket: DAILY_ATR_EXHAUSTED.
Skip R3/S3 breakout and retest signals
Skip R4/S4 breakout and retest signals
R3/S3 and R4/S4 setups (both fresh breakouts and pattern retests at those levels) are far from the daily CPR and historically unreliable. Toggle on to reject the corresponding pair entirely. R2/S2 always fires regardless. Signal Trail rejection: R3/S3 breakout/retest skipped (toggle off).
Skip if any CPR zone sits between EMA and the broken level EMA must sit within % (away from boundary = close to broken level)
Bypass this filter until (IST — momentum often outruns EMA in the first hour)
Zone count check. Counts CPR zones (S4, S3, S2, S1/PDL, CPR, R1/PDH, R2, R3, R4) strictly between the 20 EMA and the breakout level. Pair zones (CPR, R1/PDH, S1/PDL) count as one. The breakout zone itself is excluded. If 0 zones in between, the trade fires; if 1 or more, the setup is stale and skipped.

Proximity check (default 50%). The setting reads as "EMA must sit within N% away from the boundary edge" — equivalently, within the closest (100−N)% of the range from the broken level. The 100% range is measured from the broken level to the next-zone edge. Higher N = stricter (EMA must be closer to broken).
Example: BUY_ABOVE_R1_PDH with broken=R1+PDH=100, next zone above (R2)=105. Range = 5. At N=75, EMA must sit within (100−75)% = 25% of range from broken → EMA ≥ 98.75 (close to broken). EMA at 96 = 80% of range away from broken → skipped (too far). Set to 0 to disable proximity check (use zone-count only).
Candle Pattern Thresholds
Tunable thresholds for the bullish/bearish candle patterns used by the CPR retest entries: hammer / shooting star, outside reversal (engulfing), doji reversal, morning / evening star, harami, and good-size candle (catch-all). All share the retest-only model: the level must already be broken (state captured by the level-broken state machine) and the current bar must touch the level and close back in trade direction. Use higher numbers to make a pattern stricter (fewer signals, higher quality); lower numbers to loosen it.
Bar 1 and Bar 3 body in [ , ] × ATR Bar 2 body ≤ × bar 1 body
Bar 3 penetrates ≥ into bar 1 body (0.5 = 50%)
Bars 1 and 3 share the same [floor, ceiling] × ATR band — by classical convention (Nison) the outer bars are treated as symmetric impulses. Bar 2 (the "star") stays small relative to bar 1. Bar 3 penetration sets how deep into bar 1's body bar 3 must close: 0.0 = past bar 1's close (any recovery), 0.5 = past midpoint (Nison default), 1.0 = past bar 1's open (full reclaim, same as Three Inside Up). Defaults: 0.5–2.0 × ATR outer bars, 0.3 × bar1 body for the middle bar, 0.5 penetration. Set the ceiling to 0 to disable the size cap.
Bar 1 and Bar 3 body in [ , ] × ATR
Bar 3 penetrates ≥ into bar 1 body (1.0 = past bar 1 open)
Bars 1 and 3 share the same [floor, ceiling] × ATR band — symmetric with Morning/Evening Star. Floor keeps both outer bars meaningful; ceiling skips climactic exhaustion. Bar 2 opposite-color, body fully INSIDE bar 1's body — classical Harami containment, no extra size cap. Bar 3 penetration: 1.0 = past bar 1's open (classical Nison Three Inside Up/Down), 0.5 = past midpoint, 0.0 = past bar 1's close (any recovery). Defaults: 0.5–2.0 × ATR shared band, 1.0 penetration. Set any side to 0 to disable.
Bar 2 body in [ , ] × ATR
Bar 2 penetrates ≥ into bar 1 body (1.0 = classical engulfing — bar 2 closes past bar 1's open)
Classical 2-bar bullish/bearish engulfing. Strict color flip required (red→green for bullish, green→red for bearish). Body band applies only to the confirmation bar (bar 2) — bar 1 just needs to be the opposite color and be engulfed by the penetration check (bar 1's body size is unconstrained, since the pattern's geometry already requires bar 2 > bar 1). Bar 2 penetration: 1.0 = past bar 1's open (classical strict engulfing, default); 0.5 = past midpoint (accepts shallower reversals); 0.0 = past bar 1's close (any directional recovery). Defaults: 0.5–2.0 × ATR bar-2 band, 1.0 penetration. Trade-log tag: OUTSIDE_REVERSAL_RETEST.
Doji body ≤ × range Confirm body in [ , ] × ATR
Bar 1 is the doji (tiny body relative to its range). Bar 2 is the directional confirmation — body must sit inside the band [floor, ceiling] × ATR AND close past the doji's entire range: bullish reversal closes past doji's high, bearish closes past doji's low. The close-past-range rule is hardcoded (not configurable) — it ensures the reversal bar fully eclipses the indecision bar. Defaults: 0.10 × range (10%) for the doji, 0.5–2.0 × ATR for the confirmation. Set the ceiling to 0 to disable the cap.
Rejection wick ≥ × range Opposite wick ≤ × range
Single range-relative test. Rejection wick dominates the bar's total range, opposite wick stays capped. Body size is implicitly capped at (1 − rejection − opposite) × range. Defaults: 0.50 (50% rejection), 0.30 (30% opposite cap) → body ≤ 20% of range when both wicks at limits, up to 50% otherwise. Tighter: 0.60 / 0.30 for classical pin bars, or 0.67 / 0.33 for strict Pivot Boss.
Body in [ , ] × ATR
Last in the matcher chain — fires only when no stricter named pattern matched. Body must sit inside the [floor, ceiling] × ATR band — floor keeps weak bars out, ceiling kills oversized exhaustion / blow-off bars. The opposing-wick cap is controlled by the shared Confirmation Max Opposing Wick setting at the top of this section. Defaults: 0.6–2.0 × ATR body.
Slack ≤ × ATR
A retest pattern's bar can fall short of the level by up to this much ATR and still count as a touch. Absorbs tick-level whisker misses. Applies to all 6 retest patterns (hammer, outside-reversal, doji, star, harami, good-size). Set to 0 to require strict equality. Default: 0.2 × ATR.
|close − level| ≤ × ATR
Caps how far the confirmation bar's close (the trade entry) can be from the near edge of the retested level (upper edge for buy zones, lower edge for sell zones, the line itself for single-line R/S levels). Filters out patterns where bars touched the level via whisker-tolerance but the entry then ran far away, leaving a wide SL. Set to 0 to disable. Default: 1.0 × ATR.
Opposing wick ≤ × body
Applied to the confirmation bar of every pattern — bar 2 of Outside Reversal / Doji Reversal, bar 3 of Morning/Evening Star and Three Inside Up/Down, and the single-bar Good Size catch-all. For bullish patterns this caps the upper wick; for bearish, the lower wick. Default 0.5 (wick can be at most half the body). Set to 0 to disable entirely. Rejects bars whose body says "buyer/seller commitment" but whose wick says "got rejected at the extreme."
Index Alignment Filters
Reject when the stock's primary-index trend opposes the trade direction
Each stock maps to one primary index (NIFTY 50 OR a sector index) via the Stock Universe master table. This filter gates every signal against the trend state of THAT index — replaces the prior separate NIFTY + sector alignment checks.
Two-factor state machine on the primary index: CPR + EMA 20 (same logic the bot uses everywhere). States: BULLISH, BEARISH, BULLISH REVERSAL, BEARISH REVERSAL, INSIDE, SIDEWAYS, NEUTRAL.
  • Trend-following setups (HPT): buys accept BULLISH or BULLISH REVERSAL; sells accept BEARISH or BEARISH REVERSAL.
  • Counter-trend setups (magnet, mean-reversion): require strict BULLISH (buys) / BEARISH (sells). Reversal states not enough.
  • INSIDE / SIDEWAYS / opposite-direction → hard-reject as INDEX_OPPOSED.
  • NEUTRAL (no data yet) → fail-open (no skip).
Reject when the stock's primary-index 1-hour close hasn't cleared its nearest weekly-levels hurdle
Min headroom to next hurdle: × index ATR (0 = off)
Identifies the primary index's nearest weekly hurdle in trade direction and rejects the signal while the index's most-recently-completed 1-hour close hasn't decisively cleared it. 1-hour buckets are session-aligned: 9:15-10:15, 10:15-11:15, …, 14:15-15:15. Pre-10:15 IST today (Tue-Fri), falls back to the index's previous trading day session close (3:30 PM close) within the current ISO week. On Monday pre-10:15 trades with a hurdle in trade direction are REJECTED until the 10:15 close confirms.
  • Buys — nearest of {weekly R4, R3, R2, R1, PWH, weekly TC, weekly Pivot, weekly BC} below index LTP
  • Sells — nearest of {weekly TC, weekly Pivot, weekly BC, S1, PWL, S2, S3, S4} above index LTP

Headroom check. Even after the past hurdle is cleared, the filter also checks the nearest hurdle in the opposite direction. If that upcoming hurdle is closer than min headroom × index ATR, the trade is rejected. Default 1.0 × ATR. Set to 0 to disable just the headroom check. Signal Trail bucket: INDEX_HTF_HURDLE.
Reject when the stock's primary-index 5-min close is inside / hasn't fully cleared its nearest CPR / R1+PDH / S1+PDL zone
Min headroom to next hurdle: × index ATR (0 = off)
5-min variant of the Index HTF Hurdle, against the primary index's daily levels treated as zones: CPR [min(TC,BC), max(TC,BC)], R1+PDH and S1+PDL plus R2/R3/R4 and S2/S3/S4 as single-line zones. Picks the behind zone in trade direction and rejects if the index's same-bucket 5-min close is inside that zone or short of its far edge — clearing only the near edge isn't enough; the close must clear the far edge. Fail-open if the index's same-bucket bar isn't yet completed.

Headroom check. Even after the behind zone is fully cleared, the filter rejects if the next zone ahead is closer than min headroom × index ATR. Default 1.0 × ATR. Set to 0 to disable just the headroom check. Signal Trail bucket: INDEX_5M_HURDLE.
HTF Alignment Filters
Reject when the stock's own 1-hour HTF trend opposes the trade direction
Confirms the stock's slower (1-hour) timeframe agrees with the 5-min setup direction. Two-factor state machine on the stock: 1-hour close vs weekly CPR (TC/BC) + 1-hour close vs 1-hour EMA 20. Same six states as Index Trend Alignment.
  • Buy needs BULLISH or BULLISH REVERSAL.
  • Sell needs BEARISH or BEARISH REVERSAL.
  • INSIDE / SIDEWAYS / opposite-direction → hard-reject as STOCK_HTF_OPPOSED.
  • NEUTRAL (weekly levels or 1-hour close missing) → fail-open.
Reject when stock's 1-hour close hasn't cleared its nearest weekly-levels hurdle
Min headroom to next hurdle: × stock ATR (0 = off)
Same logic as the Index HTF Hurdle, but applied to each stock's own data. For a 5-min breakout, find the nearest weekly hurdle in the trade direction relative to the stock's current LTP: buys → the highest of {weekly R4, R3, R2, R1, PWH, weekly TC, weekly Pivot, weekly BC} strictly below LTP; sells → the lowest of {weekly TC, weekly Pivot, weekly BC, S1, PWL, S2, S3, S4} strictly above LTP. Then check the stock's most-recently-completed 1-hour close (session-aligned 9:15-10:15, 10:15-11:15, …): cleared → trade fires; didn't clear → reject. Pre-10:15 today (Tue-Fri) → falls back to the stock's previous trading day session close (3:30 PM close) within the current ISO week and checks if THAT cleared the level. On Monday pre-10:15 there's no current-week fallback → trades with a hurdle in trade direction are REJECTED until the 10:15 close confirms. Applies to all HPT and MPT breakouts. Fail-open when any level is warming up.

Headroom check. Even after the past hurdle is cleared, the filter also checks the nearest hurdle in the opposite direction (above LTP for buys / below for sells). If that upcoming hurdle is closer than min headroom × stock ATR, the trade is rejected. Default 1.0 × ATR. Set to 0 to disable just the headroom check.
Target Settings
Shift target to weekly CPR level when one sits between entry and chosen daily target
The target picker walks the daily CPR ladder (S4 ... R4 plus mid-points) closest-first until a level satisfies min R/R. With this on, the picker then checks all weekly CPR levels (Pivot, TC, BC, R1-R4, S1-S4, PWH, PWL) — if one sits strictly between entry and the chosen daily, the target is shifted to the weekly closest to entry and R/R is re-checked. If the shifted R/R falls below the minimum, the trade is rejected. With this off, only daily CPR levels are considered — whatever the daily walk picks becomes the final target as-is.
Discount target by ATR (places target inside the level)
Markets often reverse just before touching CPR levels. The target order is placed slightly inside the level so near-miss reversals still fill.
₹
Skip trade if expected profit (qty × target distance) is below this amount. Catches trades where stacked qty reductions made the position too small. Set to 0 to disable.

CPR Width Scanner
Each stock is classified against its own 20-day SMA of CPR width % — same percentage means different things for a low- vs high-volatility name. State decides the recommended strategy:
  • A. Narrow — widthRatio ≤ narrow threshold → volatility squeeze, breakout candidate.
  • B. Average — narrow < widthRatio ≤ wide threshold → orderly range, pullback setups at CPR / EMA.
  • C. Wide — widthRatio > wide threshold → wide CPR exhausted, mean-reversion fade.
Both thresholds are tunable above. Toggles control which states appear on the scanner watchlist. INSUFFICIENT_DATA (<20 days history) is always excluded.

Exclude stocks outside this price range. Set to 0 to disable either bound. Restart required.
Active for the next trading days (0 = disabled)
When NIFTY's session range never overlapped the daily CPR (BC..TC) by 15:30 IST, that day's CPR levels (TC / Pivot / BC) are cached as a virgin CPR for the next N trading days. A new virgin CPR replaces any existing active record. Default 10. The active virgin CPR is consumed by the Virgin CPR Hurdle Filter on the Index Alignment tab and shown as a shield icon on the NIFTY card — click for the formed-on date and exact levels.
