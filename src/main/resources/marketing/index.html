Selling ATM options, systematically, on VWAP breakdown.

A rules-based short-options system on NIFTY weekly ATM options. Two-candle trigger anchored on each option's own session VWAP. Tick-cadence stop loss. Portfolio-aware sizing that shrinks lots when risk headroom tightens. Fully automated intraday — no discretionary clicks at the terminal.

The strategy · four setups

One rule, applied to each ATM option's own VWAP.

Every 2-min bar close on the ATM CE and ATM PE runs the same trigger-candle FSM. Below-VWAP close seeds a trigger; the very next bar closing below the trigger's low sells that leg. CE and PE run independently — either, both, or neither can fire on any given session.

Bullish

H4 Breakout · sell PE

Spot closes above H4 on the confirmation candle. Momentum is confirmed upward; further downside gets progressively unlikely as the session extends.

Sell PE at strike near H3 — one level below the breakout.
Bearish

L4 Breakdown · sell CE

Spot closes below L4. The market has broken through its lower support; a large rally back above L3 becomes progressively unlikely.

Sell CE at strike near L3 — one level above the breakdown.
Bearish

H3 Reversal · sell CE

Spot pokes above H3 then closes back below. A failed test of upper resistance — session bias shifts down; a push above H4 becomes progressively unlikely.

Sell CE at strike near H4 — one level above the failed poke.
Bullish

L3 Reversal · sell PE

Spot pokes below L3 then closes back above. A failed test of lower support — session bias shifts up; a break below L4 becomes progressively unlikely.

Sell PE at strike near L4 — one level below the failed poke.

Entry mechanics · three phases

Every fire is confirmed twice before capital leaves the account.

A close below VWAP is not enough on its own — the strategy waits for the very next bar to also close below the trigger candle's own low, then sells at market. This two-bar filter is deliberate: it kills the classic single-bar mean-reversion whipsaw.

Phase 01

Setup candle

A candle closes through one of the four outer levels in the qualifying pattern. That candle's high and low become the reference range for the next phase — no order is placed yet.

Phase 02

Tick trigger

The bot watches spot tick-by-tick. Fire occurs only when spot exceeds confirm ± (ATR × 0.25) — a quarter-ATR cushion filters small wick pokes that would otherwise fire and immediately reverse.

Phase 03

Order & exits

A market or limit short is placed on the OTM option leg. Stop-loss and target are set relative to the underlying spot, not the option premium, with an ATR buffer below the confirmation extreme.

Risk management · five gates

Every fire passes through five independent risk checks.

The strategy is naked short options. That means unlimited theoretical loss on the wrong side of a gap — so the discipline that gates every order is not optional. It's the whole edge.

Portfolio daily loss cap Gate 01

A single portfolio-wide daily loss ceiling. Both instruments and all four setups share one pool. When the day's realized losses cross the cap, the strategy locks out until the next session.

Exposure projection Gate 02

Before every fire, the strategy projects combined exposed risk at stop-loss and rejects if it would breach the cap. Existing open positions are counted; new fires cannot borrow risk against unrealized profit.

50 % qty retry Gate 03

When the full-lot fire would breach the cap, the bot retries once at half lots. If the half-size fire still doesn't fit, the trade is skipped. No third try — position size never fragments below half.

Risk-to-reward floor Gate 04

Every trigger computes reward and risk in spot points against the target level and stop-loss. Trades with reward < risk × floor are rejected — even if they satisfy every other filter.

RSI regime band Gate 05

Trend setups (H4 Breakout, L4 Breakdown) require RSI in the trending band. Reversal setups (H3, L3) require RSI in the neutral chop band. Mismatch skips the fire.

Structural stop-loss Always on

Stop-loss lives on the underlying, not the premium. It sits below the confirmation low with a small ATR buffer — invalidated only when the setup thesis itself is broken by price action.

Instruments · two indices, one strategy

CE and PE in parallel.

Both ATM legs run the same trigger-candle FSM against their own independent session VWAP. Each fires without regard to the other, but they share the portfolio risk pool — a fire on one directly consumes headroom on the next.

Context

What this is, and what it isn't.

What it is

An operator's internal, automated trading system. Live-only, no simulator. Fyers broker integration via REST and WebSocket. Runs itself intraday from open to squareoff with no manual entries required.

What it isn't

Not a subscription. Not a signal service. Not financial advice. There are no accounts to open, no alerts to buy, and no plan to sell copies of the strategy. This page exists to describe how the system works.
